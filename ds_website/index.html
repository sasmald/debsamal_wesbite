03 / HOW I APPROACH THE WORK

Keep the strategy close
to the people using it.

The investment thesis and the implementation plan need to inform each other. I work across founders, clinical leaders, frontline teams, IT and operations to surface competing needs and use them to guide product scope, sequencing and adoption.

  1. Find the real constraint.

    Start with the health-system problem, the buyer and the investment thesis. Understand why the existing approach falls short.

  2. Make the product choices.

    Translate the thesis into a platform, a workable care model and a clear boundary around what to build first.

  3. Build a path to market.

    Test the commercial model and the partnership options. Make adoption requirements part of the business case.

  4. Stay through adoption.

    Work through integration, training and governance. Learn from what changes in practice, not just what ships.
