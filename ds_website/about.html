ABOUT

An engineer’s grounding.
A venture builder’s perspective.

I started by building systems, then products, then ventures. Today, my work connects healthcare product management and venture incubation: helping shape which opportunities to pursue, what to build and how to make adoption feasible.

THE THROUGH-LINE

Stay close to the problem.
Take responsibility for the path.

Engineering taught me to look beneath the surface of a problem. At Infosys, Oracle and InsideView, I worked on enterprise applications, platforms and data systems. Leading data engineering at InsideView connected technical choices with a commercial human-in-the-loop data-quality offering.

At UPMC Enterprises, the scope expanded to health-system strategy, investment theses and underwriting, venture incubation and asset commercialization. I learned alongside clinicians, founders and operators, then carried that perspective into portfolio governance.

Today, at Hashed Health, I lead product strategy across the venture studio and provide product and platform leadership within emerging companies. Working with founders and multiple venture teams, I review strategy, challenge assumptions and guide product and commercialization decisions.

Leading across teams means making the choices and tradeoffs clear. I connect clinical workflows, frontline needs, IT requirements, operating constraints and buyer priorities with the venture’s product and business strategy. That is how I approach health-system alignment: using those requirements to shape what gets built and how it reaches practice.

I also continue as Strategic Advisor to Glimmer Health’s CEO, following my work leading its incubation at UPMC Enterprises.
